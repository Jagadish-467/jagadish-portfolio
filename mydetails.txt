a large-scale Business Entity Resolution problem.

multiple sources to unified real-world entities.

TECHNIQUES

- Text normalization

- Indic-script transliteration

- Character n-grams
- Address-token matching
- Sparse candidate generation
- Blocking
- Candidate ranking

The competition involved tens of millions of records.

CONSTRAINTS

- No external data
- Open models
- Model-size restrictions
- Local computation
- MacBook Pro
- Apple MPS

REPORTED RESULT

- Final public score: approximately 0.9863 Macro F0.5
- Best rank: 135
